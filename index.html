HAVE QUESTIONS?

Good questions. Clear answers.

A few things you might want to know before starting a project.

How long does a website take?

A typical business website can take around one to three weeks, depending on its scope, content and revision requirements.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Our websites are designed to adapt to desktop and mobile screen sizes.

Can I update my website later?

Yes. Updates and additional functionality can be discussed separately after delivery.

Is domain and hosting included?

Domain registration and any paid hosting services are quoted separately unless explicitly included in your project agreement.

How do I start a project?

Send us an enquiry through WhatsApp or our contact form. We'll discuss your requirements and prepare a quotation.
